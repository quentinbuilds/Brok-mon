Instruction Booklet

Thank you for purchasing BROKÉMON. Please read this before you lose it under the couch.

This is you. Cap, beard, 16 by 16. He cannot walk diagonally. We tried.

Controls

JOYSTICKWalk. Up, down, left, right. That is the list.
ATalk. Fight. Throw the orb. Start the game.
BCancel. Run away. Pretend you meant to do that.
COpen the menu. Look at your stuff. Close the menu.

How to play

Walk into the tall grass. Something may jump you. If nothing does, walk again. This is the whole loop.

When a wild one shows up, you talk until somebody falls over. Then you throw an orb, or you leave. Both are legal. BrokéStudio does not judge. BrokéStudio is tired.

WARNING Do not insert this cart into a real Game Boy. It will not fit. We measured. If the screen goes black, that is the iris. Wait. Or press A. One of those works.
