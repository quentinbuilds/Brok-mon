01 What even is this

A top-down monster-catcher crammed into a 200×120 viewport, integer-scaled onto an 800×480 Waveshare panel, running on an Arduino UNO Q because somebody at the hackathon said "Game Boy" and BrokéStudio said yes.

That bearded guy in the cap is the actual player. 16×16 pixels. Person 6 named him cap_beard. He walks. Tall grass jumps him. He debates a wild Mossbug. He opens the menu. He remembers this was supposed to ship in twelve hours.
